Common Questions

Everything runs locally between your browser and your own computer.

Why do I need the Windows Connector?

Cloud servers cannot directly open a GST Portal login window on your desktop, read Tally Prime XML on localhost:9000, or scan your local Windows Downloads folder. The Connector bridges those actions securely from your PC.

Can I still reconcile files without downloading it?

Yes. If you already exported your GSTR-2B Excel/JSON and Purchase Register Excel/CSV files manually, you can upload them directly on the Reconciliation Hub without installing anything.

Do my GST Portal credentials leave my computer?

No. Saved GST Portal credentials and local tokens are encrypted with a machine-specific key inside your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\TallyWalls) and are never sent to cloud servers.

How do I uninstall or stop it?

Simply close the TallyWalls Connector window to stop the background bridge immediately. To remove it completely, delete the extracted TallyWallsConnector folder.
