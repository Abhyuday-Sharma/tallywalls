Why GST Reconciliation Matters to Business Cash Flow

Under Indian GST law (specifically CGST Rule 36(4)), a business can only claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) for invoices that their suppliers have officially filed in their returns and which appear in the recipient's GSTR-2B statement. Discrepancies like incorrect invoice numbers, missing vendor filings, or date mismatches cause either loss of tax credit or costly tax notices with 18% annual interest. This project automates the matching of external government portal data against internal accounting books.

The End-to-End GST Input Tax Credit Pipeline
How money and invoice data move from vendor sale to internal book reconciliation:
1

Vendor Issues Bill

Vendor sells goods/services, issues Tax Invoice with their GSTIN, buyer's GSTIN, and GST charge (e.g., ₹18,000).

2

Vendor Files GSTR-1

By the 11th of next month, vendor files outward supplies (GSTR-1) on the government GST Portal (gst.gov.in).

3

GSTR-2B Generated

On the 14th of each month, GST portal compiles all filed invoices into a static statement (GSTR-2B) for the buyer.

4

Internal Booking

Buyer's accounts team enters the invoice into Tally / ERP Purchase Register to claim purchase expense.

5

Automated Reconciliation

Our tool matches 2B vs. Books to identify eligible ITC, typos, value variances, and unfiled vendor bills.

1. GST Fundamentals & Transaction Types

4 Terms
GST (Goods & Services Tax) Statutory

A comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based indirect tax levied on the manufacture, sale, and consumption of goods and services throughout India, replacing legacy taxes like VAT, Service Tax, and Excise.

Role in Project: The entire reconciliation tool ensures compliance with GST laws and prevents wrongful claims or penalties.
Standard Tax Slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%
GSTIN Primary Key

Goods and Services Tax Identification Number: A unique 15-digit alphanumeric identifier assigned to every registered business taxpayer in India.

Role in Project: Unlike vendor names (which can be misspelled or duplicated, e.g., "Bhavik Enterprises"), the GSTIN is legally unique. It is our primary lookup key for pairing invoices.
Structure: 07 AAAAA 0000 A 1 Z 5
[07 = Delhi State Code] [10 Digits PAN] [Entity] [Z] [Check Digit]
B2B (Business-to-Business) Transaction

Commercial sales where both the selling party and the purchasing party hold active GSTIN numbers. In B2B transactions, the buyer pays GST to the seller and is entitled to claim Input Tax Credit.

Role in Project: This tool reconciles B2B purchases exclusively, because only B2B invoices confer ITC benefits to the buyer.
Supplier GSTIN: 07AAACB1234F1Z1 ➔ Buyer GSTIN: 08BBBCD5678K1Z2
B2C (Business-to-Consumer / Cash) Transaction

Sales made to an end consumer or an unregistered purchaser without a GSTIN. Often referred to in everyday business as "Cash Sales" or retail billing.

Role in Project: No ITC is passed to the buyer in B2C transactions. These do not require GSTR-2B purchase reconciliation.
Sweet shop or retail store bill issued without customer GSTIN.

2. GST Return Forms & Government Statements

4 Terms
GSTR-1 Government Form

The monthly or quarterly return filed by suppliers to report all their outward supplies (sales) and tax collected from buyers. Due by the 11th of each following month.

Role in Project: If a vendor fails to file their GSTR-1, their invoice will never reflect in the buyer's 2B statement, blocking the buyer from claiming ITC.
Vendor files GSTR-1 for October by 11th November.
GSTR-2B Statutory Baseline

An auto-drafted, static Input Tax Credit (ITC) statement generated on the 14th of every month for the buyer, based on all GSTR-1s filed by suppliers up to the cutoff date.

Role in Project: GSTR-2B is the legal foundation of our tool. Under Indian tax rules, buyers cannot claim more ITC than what appears in this document.
October 2B statement is finalized on 14th November and never changes.
GSTR-2A vs GSTR-2B Comparison

GSTR-2A is dynamic (updates continuously whenever a vendor files late), whereas GSTR-2B is static (locks in month-by-month for exact tax calculation).

Role in Project: Tax authorities mandate reconciliation against GSTR-2B (not 2A) for monthly tax filing in GSTR-3B.
GSTR-2A: Dynamic view | GSTR-2B: Statutory benchmark for claim
GSTR-3B Final Filing

The self-declared monthly summary return filed by the 20th of every month, where businesses summarize their total sales, eligible ITC, and pay their net GST tax liability to the government.

Role in Project: The output figures produced by our reconciliation tool feed directly into Table 4 of GSTR-3B.
Net Tax Paid = Total Output Tax (Sales) - Eligible ITC (from 2B Reconciled)

3. Accounting Records, Tax Components & ITC Rules

5 Terms
Purchase Register (Books) Internal Source

The internal ledger maintained by the company's accounting team in software like Tally Prime, Busy, SAP, or Zoho Books, listing all physical bills received from vendors.

Role in Project: Represents "File 2" in our reconciliation engine. It tells us what purchases the business claims to have made.
Exported from Tally Prime as Excel / CSV with bill numbers & dates.
ITC (Input Tax Credit) Financial Asset

The tax credit a business receives for GST paid on purchasing goods or services used in the course of business, which can be deducted from the GST payable on outward sales.

Role in Project: ITC is actual money. Every ₹1 of matched ITC reduces ₹1 of cash tax outflow for the company.
Sales Tax Payable: ₹50,000 | Reconciled ITC: ₹35,000 | Net Cash Paid: ₹15,000
Taxable Value vs Total Invoice Value Calculation

Taxable Value is the base price of goods/services before GST is applied. Total Invoice Value is the final bill amount including GST.

Role in Project: GST rates (e.g. 18%) apply strictly to the Taxable Value. Matching taxable value prevents mismatches caused by freight, discounts, or rounding.
Base Taxable: ₹1,00,000 + GST (18%): ₹18,000 = Total Invoice Value: ₹1,18,000
CGST, SGST & IGST Tax Breakdown

* CGST + SGST: Levied equally on Intra-state sales (within same state, e.g., Delhi to Delhi: 9% Central + 9% State = 18%).
* IGST: Levied as a single tax on Inter-state sales (e.g., Haryana to Delhi: 18% Integrated Tax).

Role in Project: In accounting, IGST cannot always be mixed with CGST/SGST without set-off priority rules. The tool verifies component-level accuracy.
Intra-State: CGST ₹9,000 + SGST ₹9,000 | Inter-State: IGST ₹18,000
Rule 36(4) of CGST Rules Strict Law

A statutory amendment in Indian GST rules that mandates zero tolerance: No taxpayer can claim ITC unless the supplier's invoice is visible in GSTR-2B.

Role in Project: This legal rule makes software-driven GSTR-2B reconciliation a non-negotiable monthly necessity for every Indian company.
Unmatched bill claimed in 3B = Automated ASMT-10 scrutiny notice.

4. Reconciliation Engine & Discrepancy Categorization

5 Terms
Exact Match Green (Ready)

A scenario where the supplier's GSTIN, Invoice Number, Taxable Value, and GST Tax Amount match 100% between GSTR-2B and Books.

Action: Approved automatically for full ITC claim in the current tax period.
Portal: GSTIN-A | Bill #104 | Taxable ₹50,000 | Tax ₹9,000
Books: GSTIN-A | Bill #104 | Taxable ₹50,000 | Tax ₹9,000 ➔ 100% Match
Normalized / Fuzzy Match Yellow (Format)

A match where GSTIN and amounts match identically, but the invoice number has formatting noise such as leading zeroes (0049 vs 49), slashes (/), hyphens (-), or prefixes (INV).

Action: Auto-cleaned by stripping special characters and non-significant zeros, saving hours of manual Excel editing.
Portal: "00049" ➔ Cleaned: "49"
Books: "INV/2023-24/49" ➔ Cleaned: "49" ➔ Auto-Matched!
Probable Match (Invoice Typo) Yellow (Typo)

A scenario where the GSTIN and Taxable/Tax amounts match to the penny, but the invoice number has human typing errors (e.g. inverted digits like 49 vs 94).

Action: Flagged in the UI for a 1-click human confirmation: "Amounts match perfectly; confirm if this is a typo in Bill Number."
Vendor: 07AAA... | Bill "94" | Tax ₹18,450
Books: 07AAA... | Bill "49" | Tax ₹18,450 ➔ Flagged as Probable Typo
ITC at Risk (Missing in GSTR-2B) Red Alert

Invoices recorded in the company's Books, but completely absent from the government's GSTR-2B portal because the vendor did not file their return or entered the wrong GSTIN.

Action: ITC cannot be claimed. The tool compiles an automated vendor follow-up list to demand immediate GSTR-1 filing before making vendor payment.
₹2,40,000 paid to cement vendor, but vendor did not file GSTR-1.
Unbooked ITC (Missing in Books) Blue Notice

Invoices appearing in GSTR-2B on the government portal that the internal accounts department forgot to record in Tally/ERP.

Action: Notifies the internal accounts department to enter the bill into Tally before the financial year-end deadline to avoid losing legitimate credit.
Utility / Courier bill of ₹1,500 present on portal but missing in Tally.
